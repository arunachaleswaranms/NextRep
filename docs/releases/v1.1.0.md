# NextRep v1.1.0

More control over hard days, progress you can share, six new habits and
twice as many achievements. Still private, local-first and offline.

**Updating from v1.0.0? Install over it. Don't uninstall first.** See
[Updating from v1.0.0](#updating-from-v100).

## What's new

### More control over your day

- **Minimum Day can be undone.** Switched to Minimum Day and changed your
  mind? Tap **Undo** on the message, or **Return to full targets** on the
  banner, any time that day. Your progress stays; completion and XP are
  worked out again against the full targets. Past days never change.
- An achievement unlocked today follows today's progress, so undoing a
  habit or a Minimum Day can take it back. Earlier unlocks never change.
- **Sleep Before Target is clearer.** It now reads as "Last night's
  bedtime": in the morning, log when you went to sleep, and it counts for
  today. Your own clock-time habits keep neutral wording.

### Share your progress

Make a picture of your progress and send it wherever you like:

- **Today's Progress**: today's completion, your habits, and optionally
  your XP & level and streak.
- **My Arc**: your Arc and the goals you've committed to.
- **Achievement**: any achievement you've unlocked, with the day you
  earned it.

Tap **Share progress** on Today, or open an unlocked achievement. On
Today's Progress you can hide habit names, XP & level and your streak
before sharing.

Cards are 1080 × 1350 images made on your phone. Tap **Share** and your
phone's own share sheet opens, so you choose the app. NextRep never posts
anything by itself, and there's no account or friends list. Cards carry a
subtle NextRep name; neither the image nor its short caption includes a
link.

Motion: the Minimum Day change now eases in, and the share preview reveals
once. With reduced motion on, both are instant.

### More ways to build your Arc

Six new optional habits when you set up an Arc:

- Hair Care
- Skin Care
- Eat Fruit
- Nuts / Seeds
- Stretch / Mobility
- Reading

### More achievements

**32 achievements** (up from 15), grouped in six categories: Progress,
Consistency, Resilience, Habit Mastery, Reflection and Journey.

- Longer streaks (14, 30 and 50 days) and 25 Perfect Days.
- Resilience: seven Minimum Days.
- Reflection: 14 and 30 reflections.
- Journey: Day 14, Day 60 and Day 75.
- Habit milestones for No Junk Food, Workout, Water, Learning and Sleep
  Before.

Your existing history counts. On first launch, NextRep checks it against
the new achievements and unlocks the ones you've already earned, dated the
day you earned them. Your current Arc's new unlocks are celebrated
together, once. Past Arcs are filled in quietly. Achievements still give
no XP, so your XP and level don't change.

## Privacy

- Local-first: your data stays in the app's storage on your phone.
- No account, no analytics, no ads, no backend, no cloud sync.
- The Android release still doesn't request the Internet permission.
- Share cards are made on your phone and leave it only when you tap Share
  and pick an app in the system share sheet. They never include your
  Journal, moods, reminder settings or a logged bedtime, and they carry no
  link: only a subtle NextRep name. Sharing needs no storage, photo or
  contacts permission.
- Once shared, a card is up to the app you chose, which has its own
  privacy practices.

Details: [PRIVACY.md](../../PRIVACY.md).

## Updating from v1.0.0

**Do not uninstall v1.0.0.** Uninstalling deletes your NextRep data.

1. Recommended: open **Data & Backup** (from Arc History) and tap
   **Export Backup**. Save the `.nextrep` file somewhere safe, off the
   phone if you can.
2. Download `NextRep-v1.1.0-android.apk` from this release.
3. Open it and tap **Update**. It installs over v1.0.0.
4. Open NextRep. Your Arc, habits, progress, XP, Journal and history are
   all there.

v1.1.0 is signed with the same certificate as v1.0.0, so Android checks the
update and keeps the app's data. The database is unchanged (schema v5), so
nothing is migrated.

If Android says the update conflicts with the installed app, or that the
package is invalid, **stop. Don't uninstall to force it.** Download the APK
again from this page and check its checksum and certificate (below).

New install? Follow
[Install on Android](../../README.md#install-on-android).

## New habit limitation

The six new habits can be chosen when you set up an Arc, up to the usual 12
habits. They are **not** added to an Arc that's already running, and v1.1.0
can't add a new habit to a running Arc. They'll be there when you set up
your next one.

## Backup compatibility

| Backup from | Restore into | |
|---|---|---|
| v1.0.0 | v1.1.0 | Yes |
| v1.1.0 | v1.1.0 | Yes |
| v1.1.0 | v1.0.0 | Not supported. A backup with a new achievement is refused by v1.0.0 |

Backups are still format 2, and v1.1.0 reads formats 1 and 2. Going back
to an older version isn't supported. Backup files are **not encrypted** by
NextRep: keep them somewhere you trust.

## Compatibility

- Requires Android 7.0 (API 24) or newer, on arm64, armv7 or x86_64 devices.
- Tested as an update over v1.0.0 on a physical OnePlus CPH2707 (Android
  16), and on the Android 16 (API 36) emulator. Other devices are expected
  to work but weren't tested.
- iPhone: no build is published. The source builds for iOS.
- Data: database schema v5. Backups are written as format 2; format 1 and 2
  backups can be restored.
