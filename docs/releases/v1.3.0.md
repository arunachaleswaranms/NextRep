# NextRep v1.3.0

> **Status: release candidate, not yet published.** These notes were
> prepared during Phase 12I release qualification. The values under
> [Verification](#verification) were measured on the qualified candidate.
> The physical-phone result is still **pending** (Gate I). Until a GitHub
> Release named v1.3.0 exists, v1.2.0 is the latest published version.

**Arc Momentum.** See how your Arc is going week by week and day by day,
put your habits in your own order, pause and resume a Rolling Arc,
restart cleanly, and hear a short sound when you finish a habit. Still
private, local-first and offline: no new permission, account or network
request.

**Updating from v1.2.0? Install over it. Don't uninstall first.** See
[Updating from v1.2.0](#updating-from-v120).

## What's new

### Weekly Arc Review

- Open **Week N review** from the Journey to see one week of your Arc:
  Days 1–7, 8–14, … and an 8-day summit week (Days 85–92).
- Habit completion, consistency, XP, Perfect Days and Minimum Days for the
  days that are over. Today's progress is shown apart until the day ends.
- The habit that carried the week, one that could use attention, and a
  short, supportive summary.
- Works for the current Arc and for finished Arcs in Arc History.
- **Read-only:** viewing a review never changes XP, progress or
  achievements. Nothing is stored or sent.

### Arc Consistency Calendar

- A **Path | Calendar** switch on the Journey shows the same 92 days as
  13 Arc-week blocks (Week 13 holds 8 days).
- Each day uses the Journey's own states: Perfect, Minimum complete,
  partial, no progress, today, upcoming, before you joined and not
  reached. Pauses show as a row between days.
- Each week's heading shows its dates and how many finished days were
  full, and opens that week's review.
- Read-only, like the review.

### Habit order, saved setup and Confirm & Start

- **Put your habits in order** before an Arc starts: drag a card by its
  handle, or use **Move up** / **Move down** (also available to screen
  readers). That order is used on Today, the Journey and the Weekly
  Review. A running Arc's order stays fixed.
- **Your setup is saved as you edit it.** **Save for Later** opens a hub
  with Start Challenge, Review Setup, Continue Editing and **Cancel
  Setup**. NextRep reopens there while a setup exists.
- **Review Setup** and **Confirm & Start**: before an Arc starts you see
  its habits, goals, Minimum Day goals, the habits that are off and your
  reminders, then confirm on a screen showing Day 1 and Day 92 (or the
  season day you join on). A Rolling Arc's Day 1 is the day you confirm.

### Pause and Resume (Rolling Arcs)

- **Pause Arc** (Today ⋮ or Habits) pauses an active Rolling Arc **from
  tomorrow**. Today still counts. Until midnight you can **Cancel pause**.
- **Paused days are never missed days.** Nothing can be logged, and
  reminders stop. Focus Guard keeps its own schedule.
- **Resume Arc** makes today the next challenge day. Your last day moves
  later by the number of paused days. Summary and Arc History show the
  days paused.
- Not offered for a Seasonal Arc, on Day 92, or while your phone's date is
  earlier than the Arc's history.

### Restart, and optional deletion of the old Arc

- **Restart with this setup** (from Habits, or the paused Today) starts a
  new Rolling 92-Day Arc on Day 1 today with the same habits, goals,
  on/off states and order. The screen says how many habits you have and
  how many are on, for example "7 habits (4 on)". Type **RESTART** to
  confirm.
- The current Arc is **kept** in Arc History as "Ended early · Day N of
  92", with its progress, XP, achievements, reflections and goal history
  exactly as they were. It is never called completed and never earns the
  Summit. A pause that was scheduled or in effect ends with it.
- **Deleting the old Arc is optional and separate.** After a restart you
  can choose "Delete my old Arc…". A separate screen lists exactly what
  will be lost and offers a backup first. Nothing is deleted unless you
  type **DELETE**. The same screen deletes any finished Arc from its
  summary.
- Restarting on the day you resumed a paused Arc works, and the old Arc
  keeps its pause in Arc History.

### Completion sounds

- A short, original sound when a saved habit action **completes** a
  habit, and a longer **Perfect Day** sound for the completion that makes
  today perfect.
- Three sets: **Ice Crystal** (the default), **Summit Spark** and **Warm
  Ember**. Choose one, preview it, or turn sounds off in **Sound &
  Feedback** (Today, beside Reminders; also from the saved-setup hub, Arc
  History and About). Sounds are **on** by default.
- Nothing plays for steps below a target, undo, Minimum Day, level-ups,
  achievements, opening the app, midnight or a restore. Repeated taps
  never queue up sounds.
- Sounds follow your phone: **quiet on silent, vibrate and Do Not
  Disturb**, and your music keeps playing.
- The sounds are bundled in the app, generated for NextRep from source in
  this repository. Nothing is downloaded.

### Smaller changes

- Easier-to-read primary and destructive buttons (dark text on blue and
  on red).
- A past day with no progress reads "No progress" instead of "Missed".
- Today's header moves its buttons to a second row on narrow screens or
  with large text.

## Your data on the update

- Your Arcs, habits, goal history, progress, XP, streaks, achievements,
  Journal, Arc History, reminders, Hydration Coach and Focus Guard
  settings are kept.
- On first launch NextRep upgrades its database from schema v6 to **v7**.
  The upgrade only **adds**: room for pauses, the last day of an Arc ended
  early, and your sound settings. No existing record is changed, and
  nothing you have logged is re-dated or re-scored.
- Completion sounds start **on** with Ice Crystal. Your reminders,
  Hydration Coach and Focus Guard stay exactly as you set them. An update
  is not a restore: nothing is switched off.
- Focus Guard's background check resumes once you have opened v1.3.0 for
  the first time.

## Backups

- Export Backup now writes **format 4**, which adds pauses, Arcs ended
  early and your sound settings.
- v1.3.0 restores backups from every earlier version: **formats 1, 2, 3
  and 4**. An older backup keeps this phone's sound setting.
- A restore turns every reminder, Hydration Coach and Focus Guard
  **off**, keeping their settings so you can turn them on again. It never
  plays a sound, and it never awards XP or achievements.
- **A v1.3.0 backup can't be restored into v1.2.0 or earlier.** They
  refuse a format-4 file as made by a newer version and change nothing.
- Backup files stay wherever you save them. They are **not encrypted**:
  keep them somewhere you trust.

| Backup from | Restore into v1.3.0 | Restore into v1.2.0 |
|---|---|---|
| v1.0.0, v1.1.0 (formats 1, 2) | Yes | Yes |
| v1.2.0 (format 3) | Yes | Yes |
| v1.3.0 (format 4) | Yes | **No**: refused, nothing changed |

## Privacy

- Local-first: your data stays in the app's storage on your phone. No
  account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, no backend, no cloud sync.
- **No new permission, dependency or network request.** The Android
  permissions are the same seven as v1.2.0
  ([PRIVACY.md](../../PRIVACY.md#permissions-android-release-build)).
- The only network request is still the update check you start by
  tapping **Check for updates** in About.
- Weekly Review, Calendar, ordering, saved setup, Pause and Resume,
  Restart and deletion all work on data already on your phone. Completion
  sounds are bundled; only your on/off choice and set are stored.

Details: [PRIVACY.md](../../PRIVACY.md).

## Updating from v1.2.0

**Do not uninstall v1.2.0.** Uninstalling deletes your NextRep data.

1. **Export a backup first.** Open **Data & Backup** (from Arc History)
   and tap **Export Backup**. Save the `.nextrep` file somewhere safe, off
   the phone if you can. (Your v1.2.0 backup can be restored into v1.3.0
   later if you ever need it.)
2. Download `NextRep-v1.3.0-android.apk` from this release.
3. Optional: check its checksum and signing certificate (below).
4. Open the APK and tap **Update**. It installs over v1.2.0.
5. Open NextRep. Your Arc, habits, progress, XP, Journal and history are
   all there.

v1.3.0 is signed with the same certificate as v1.0.0, v1.1.0 and v1.2.0.
Android checks that before it updates the app, and keeps the app's data.

If Android says the update conflicts with the installed app, or that the
package is invalid, **stop. Don't uninstall to force it.** Download the APK
again from this page and check its checksum and certificate.

Going back to v1.2.0 isn't supported, and v1.2.0 can't read a v1.3.0
backup.

New install? Follow
[Install on Android](../../README.md#install-on-android).

## Compatibility and known limitations

- Requires Android 7.0 (API 24) or newer, on arm64, armv7 or x86_64
  devices. Target SDK 36.
- Tested as a signed update over the published v1.2.0, with synthetic
  data, on the Android 16 (API 36) emulator: every existing record was
  kept unchanged. **Physical-phone qualification: pending** (Gate I).
- Completion sounds stay quiet whenever your phone is on silent, vibrate
  or Do Not Disturb. How they mix with a screen reader's speech, with
  headphones or Bluetooth, and on a particular phone's audio settings
  has not been confirmed by a person listening on a phone yet.
- Paused days are based on your phone's date. If the date is set
  backwards, Pause, Resume and Restart refuse to change anything until it
  is correct again.
- After the update, Focus Guard's background check waits until you have
  opened NextRep once.
- On some phones (OxygenOS among them) reminders and Focus Guard resume a
  few minutes after a restart, or as soon as you open NextRep.
- The reorder list's short drag animation still plays with reduced motion
  turned on. Move up / Move down is the motion-free alternative.
- iPhone: no build is published. The source builds for iOS, without Focus
  Guard or update checks.
- Data: database schema v7. Backups are written as format 4; formats 1,
  2, 3 and 4 can be restored.
