# NextRep v1.2.0

A gentle Hydration Coach, an optional Focus Guard for time spent in other
apps, and an About screen that can tell you when a new version is out.
Everything new is off until you turn it on. Still private, local-first and
offline.

**Updating from v1.1.0? Install over it. Don't uninstall first.** See
[Updating from v1.1.0](#updating-from-v110).

## What's new

### Hydration Coach

An optional coach that helps you keep pace with your Water habit through
the day.

- **Off by default.** Turn it on in **Reminders** (the bell on Today).
  NextRep asks for notification permission only when you turn it on.
- It follows your Arc's built-in **Water** habit: its goal, its Minimum Day
  target and any goal change. A custom habit you named "Water" isn't used.
- Three styles: **Gentle** (up to 3 nudges a day), **Balanced** (up to 4,
  the default) and **Supportive** (up to 5).
- **Active hours** you choose (08:00 – 20:00 by default; any same-day
  window of 8 to 18 hours). Nothing arrives outside them.
- It nudges only when it would help: when you're behind your pace, not
  when you're already ahead, and not right after you've logged water.
  Logging water updates the plan straight away.
- Nudges are local notifications ("Hydration check"), planned on your phone.
  Tapping one opens Today. Android may deliver them a few minutes late.
- Hydration Coach reminders do not award XP or change streaks. Logging
  your Water habit continues to earn its normal rewards.

### Focus Guard (Android)

An optional reminder for when the apps you choose reach a daily budget.

- **Off by default.** Open it from the Focus Guard card on Today, or from
  Arc History.
- **You choose the apps** (from the apps on your home screen) and **one
  combined daily budget** for them together: 60 minutes by default, from 1
  minute to 24 hours.
- Today shows how much of the budget you've used.
- It needs Android's **Usage Access**, which only you can switch on, on a
  system settings page NextRep opens for you. You can switch it off there
  at any time; Focus Guard then pauses. Notification permission is
  separate: without it Focus Guard still shows your time but can't send its
  reminder.
- Android runs Focus Guard's check in the background when it can, usually
  about every 15 minutes, sometimes later. It sends **at most one reminder
  per day**, when the budget is reached.
- **It never blocks, closes or covers any app.** It uses no Accessibility
  service, overlay or VPN.
- Your app usage stays on your phone. NextRep stores only your choices (the
  apps, the budget, on or off) and the date of the last reminder: no usage
  history, and nothing is uploaded.
- It has **no effect on XP, streaks, achievements or Perfect Days**.
- Not available on iPhone.

### About NextRep and update checks

- **About NextRep** (the ⓘ button in Arc History, or the About NextRep
  link on onboarding, Arc setup and a finished Arc's summary) shows the
  version and build actually installed, how NextRep handles your data, and
  the official source.
- On Android, **Check for updates** asks GitHub for the latest published
  NextRep release and tells you whether a newer one is out. If it is,
  **View official release** opens its GitHub page in your browser.
- It checks **only when you tap it**: never at startup, in the background
  or on a schedule. It never downloads or installs anything. You still
  download the APK yourself and install it over your current NextRep.
- Releases before v1.2.0 can't check. From v1.2.0 on, About tells you when
  the next one is published.

## Your data on the update

- Your Arcs, habits, progress, XP, streaks, achievements, Journal and Arc
  History are kept. On first launch NextRep upgrades its database from
  schema v5 to **v6**, adding room for the new settings. Nothing you've
  logged changes.
- Hydration Coach and Focus Guard start **off**. Your daily and reflection
  reminders stay exactly as you set them.

## Backups

- Export Backup now writes **format 3**, which adds Hydration Coach's
  active hours and style and, if you use it, Focus Guard's apps and budget.
- v1.2.0 restores backups from every earlier version (formats 1, 2 and 3).
- A restore turns every reminder, Hydration Coach and Focus Guard **off**,
  keeping their settings so you can turn them on again. It never asks for
  a permission.
- Backup files stay on your phone, or wherever you save them. NextRep
  never uploads them. They are **not encrypted**: keep them somewhere you
  trust. If you use Focus Guard, a backup includes the package names of the
  apps you chose, never any usage time.

| Backup from | Restore into v1.2.0 | Restore into v1.1.0 |
|---|---|---|
| v1.0.0, v1.1.0 (formats 1, 2) | Yes | Yes |
| v1.2.0 (format 3) | Yes | No: v1.1.0 refuses it as made by a newer version, and changes nothing |

## Privacy

- Local-first: your data stays in the app's storage on your phone. No
  account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, no backend, no cloud sync.
- The core app works fully offline.
- v1.2.0 adds Android's `INTERNET` permission for **one thing only**: the
  update check you start by tapping **Check for updates**. Each tap sends
  one HTTPS request to GitHub's public release API
  (`api.github.com/repos/arunachaleswaranms/NextRep/releases/latest`),
  with none of your NextRep data and no account or token. Like any website,
  GitHub sees the request's usual details, such as your IP address, so it
  isn't anonymous to GitHub.
- Hydration Coach, Focus Guard, reminders, backups and restores never use
  the network. Focus Guard's usage information never leaves your phone.
- New permissions, each explained in [PRIVACY.md](../../PRIVACY.md):
  `INTERNET` (the update check), `PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS` (Focus Guard's
  Usage Access, which you grant in system settings) and `WAKE_LOCK`
  (Android's background-work library, for Focus Guard's check).

Details: [PRIVACY.md](../../PRIVACY.md).

## Updating from v1.1.0

**Do not uninstall v1.1.0.** Uninstalling deletes your NextRep data.

1. **Export a backup first.** Open **Data & Backup** (from Arc History)
   and tap **Export Backup**. Save the `.nextrep` file somewhere safe, off
   the phone if you can.
2. Download `NextRep-v1.2.0-android.apk` from this release.
3. Optional: check its checksum and signing certificate (below).
4. Open the APK and tap **Update**. It installs over v1.1.0.
5. Open NextRep. Your Arc, habits, progress, XP, Journal and history are
   all there.

v1.2.0 is signed with the same certificate as v1.0.0 and v1.1.0. Android
checks that before it updates the app, and keeps the app's data.

If Android says the update conflicts with the installed app, or that the
package is invalid, **stop. Don't uninstall to force it.** Download the APK
again from this page and check its checksum and certificate.

Going back to v1.1.0 isn't supported.

New install? Follow
[Install on Android](../../README.md#install-on-android).

## Compatibility

- Requires Android 7.0 (API 24) or newer, on arm64, armv7 or x86_64
  devices.
- Tested as an update over the published v1.1.0, with its data, on the
  Android 16 (API 36) emulator and on a OnePlus CPH2707 (Android 16,
  OxygenOS) with real data: the update kept everything, and Hydration
  Coach, Focus Guard, reminders and the update check worked. Other devices
  are expected to work but weren't tested.
- On some phones (OxygenOS among them) reminders and Focus Guard resume a
  few minutes after a restart, when the phone lets NextRep start, or as
  soon as you open NextRep.
- iPhone: no build is published. The source builds for iOS, without Focus
  Guard or update checks.
- Data: database schema v6. Backups are written as format 3; format 1, 2
  and 3 backups can be restored.
